# Arnold-Palmer Regional (LBE)
> [LBE 7110.2E](../../authority-sections/7110.2E-authority.mdd)
> - Current Revision E
> - Effective Date: 2025/01/15

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    - IFR Departure Altitude: 5,000
    - IFR Departure Heading: Runway Heading
    - VFR Altitude: AOB 5,000
    - Pattern Altitude: 2,200
    - Missed Approach: 5,000 and Runway Heading

## LBE

### Positions
| Callsign | Position | Radio Name | ID | Frequency |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| LBE_GND | Ground | Palmer Ground | GC | 121.800 |
| LBE_TWR | Local | Palmer Tower | LC | 125.000 |

### Overlying Positions
The position directly overlying LBE is CLE_66_CTR (125.450) or whichever controller has responsibility over the position.


### Operational Hours
0615-2200L


### General Operations
1. LBE surface area of responsibility is defined as a 5 NM radius around LBE from surface up to and including 3700ft MSL.
2. ZOB may transition aircraft through LBE Class D airspace at or above 3,700ft without further coordination.


### Departures
1. Assign departure aircraft 5,000ft and advise them to expect requested altitude ten (10) minutes after departure unless directed otherwise by ZOB.
2. Hold all aircraft (Hold For Release) until released by ZOB.
3. Release aircraft on runway heading unless directed otherwise by ZOB.



### Arrivals
1. Assign missed approach aircraft 5,000ft and runway heading, or 20 degrees left / right as needed
2. Inform ZOB of an unplanned missed approach prior to the aircraft reaching one (1) NM from the runway departure end.
